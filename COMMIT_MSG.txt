Fix /about prerender: map SitePage bodyHtml into the [page] route

app/[page]/page.tsx read page.body but the local catalog stores static
pages as SitePage objects (field bodyHtml), so getPage() returned an object
with no body and Prose's dangerouslySetInnerHTML got undefined, crashing
the static prerender of /about. Read the page directly from lib/site
(pageByHandle) and render its bodyHtml, with SEO fallbacks.
